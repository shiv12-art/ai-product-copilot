# AI Product Copilot - Enhanced Edition

A lightweight, single-file HTML application that generates professional product strategy artifacts in seconds.

## ✨ Features

- **7 Professional Artifacts**: Product Canvas, PRD, GTM Strategy, Features, Validation Plan, Competitive Analysis, Pitch Deck
- **75+ Personas**: Industry-specific buyer personas with pain points and budgets
- **27+ Real Competitors**: Market analysis with real companies and pricing
- **14 Product Categories**: SaaS, AI Tool, FinTech, Healthcare, Mobile App, and more
- **Dark Mode Support**: Full dark/light theme toggle
- **Offline First**: Works completely offline in your browser
- **Fast**: <2 second artifact generation
- **Small**: 45KB single HTML file

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser
2. Enter your product idea
3. Select a category
4. Click "Generate All 7 Artifacts"
5. Copy, share, or download your artifacts

## 💡 Usage

**Create Artifact**:
1. Describe your product idea in the text area
2. Select a product category
3. Click "Generate All 7 Artifacts"
4. All 7 artifacts will be generated with category-specific content

**Copy Artifacts**:
- Click "Copy Selected Artifact" to copy current artifact
- Click "Copy All Artifacts" to copy all 7 artifacts
- Paste into your document/email

**Dark Mode**:
- Click the "🌙 Dark Mode" button in the header
- Theme preference is saved to localStorage

## 📚 Knowledge Base Included

### Personas (75+)
- 5-7 detailed personas per category
- Pain points, budgets, success metrics
- Real job titles and contexts

### Competitors (27+)
- 2-3 real competitors per category
- Positioning, pricing, features
- Strengths, weaknesses, our advantages

### Features
- Category-specific feature recommendations
- Implementation priorities (MVP vs Advanced)
- Business value and effort ratings

### GTM Strategies
- Market-specific go-to-market approaches
- Distribution channels
- Pricing models
- Sales strategies
